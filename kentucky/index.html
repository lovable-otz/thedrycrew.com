Water Damage and Mold in Kentucky

The State Position

There is no state certification for mold remediation companies in Kentucky. What exists instead is a set of statutory minimum standards under KRS 367.83801 to 367.83807 and 040 KAR 2:330: a company must ensure its principals and employees have the training, education and experience for the work, covering safety and health, engineering controls, containment and work practices.

Two provisions matter directly to a homeowner. A mold remediation company may not perform work without a written contract, and where a company breaches the statute the Attorney General may bring civil action and recover investigation and litigation costs. No licence to check, but a real obligation and a real enforcer.

Source: Kentucky Office of the Attorney General, KRS 367.83801 to 367.83807 and 040 KAR 2:330. Checked 2026-08-15.

How to Check a Contractor Here

Statutory duties rather than a credential. Kentucky imposes minimum standards on the work itself, so the contract and the standard are what you enforce against. Get the scope in writing, name the standard it follows, and keep it - in a standards state the paperwork is the protection.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kentucky license mold remediation?

Kentucky issues no mold licence but does impose statutory duties, including a written contract, enforced by the Attorney General.

There is no licence in Kentucky. What protects me?

Statutory duties rather than a credential. Kentucky imposes minimum standards on the work itself, so the contract and the standard are what you enforce against. Get the scope in writing, name the standard it follows, and keep it - in a standards state the paperwork is the protection.

Who sets the rules in Kentucky?

Kentucky Office of the Attorney General, under the KRS 367.83801 to 367.83807 and 040 KAR 2:330. Position checked 2026-08-15. Kentucky can change its rules, so confirm current requirements with Kentucky Office of the Attorney General before relying on this for a decision.
