Where The Dry Crew Covers

State-by-state mold licensing rules and local restoration costs. All 19 states published so far are confirmed against their own state authority.

Arizona

Arizona has no mold licence; what applies is the ordinary contractor licence, required once labour and materials exceed $1,000

California

California has no licensing requirement for mold work at all, and CDPH says so directly

Colorado

Colorado does not regulate mold at all: CDPHE says the inspection and remediation industries are completely unregulated

Connecticut

Connecticut licenses nobody for mold work, but its health department publishes abatement guidelines a contractor should be following

District of Columbia

The District licenses mold assessors and remediators separately, and requires $1,000,000 of cover from each

Florida

Florida licenses mold assessors and mold remediators separately, and the licence is required above ten square feet

Georgia

Georgia does not regulate mold work: the state health department says indoor air quality is not a regulated programme

Illinois

Illinois registers mold remediation firms rather than licensing them, and requires third-party certification to register

Kentucky

Kentucky issues no mold licence but does impose statutory duties, including a written contract, enforced by the Attorney General

Louisiana

Louisiana requires a Mold Remediation licence from the state contractor board once the job value exceeds $7,500

Maine

Maine sets statutory standards for mold work and requires a certified assessor once visible mold exceeds 3 square feet in a rental

New Hampshire

New Hampshire is the state that certifies the mold assessor and not the remediator, required for residential assessment since 1 January 2016

New York

New York licenses four separate mold roles under Article 32, and bars the same person from both assessing and remediating the same property

North Carolina

North Carolina has no mold credential; the general contractor licence only bites once a project reaches $40,000

Oregon

Oregon requires a CCB contractor licence to do mold cleanup, but there is no mold-specific credential behind it

Tennessee

Tennessee carries mold remediation as a specialty environmental classification inside the state contractor licence

Texas

Texas licenses mold work once growth reaches 25 contiguous square feet, and the consultant who writes the protocol may not be the contractor who does the work

Virginia

Virginia makes uncertified residential mold remediation a Consumer Protection Act violation rather than an unlicensed-trade offence

Washington

Washington has no mold certification requirement at all, and the Department of Health says so in as many words
