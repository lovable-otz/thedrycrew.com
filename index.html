How Pricing Works

Why Category Sets the Price

The IICRC S500 standard sorts water into three categories, and the category drives the cost far more than the size of the room does. Clean water from a supply line dries at one rate. Water carrying contamination costs roughly double. Sewage or floodwater roughly triples it, because porous materials that have been in contact with it are removed and replaced rather than dried.

Category also degrades with time. Water that was clean on day one can be Category 2 within a day or two and Category 3 after that, which is the mechanism by which a cheap job becomes an expensive one while nobody is doing anything.

The 24-48 Hour Window

The Mold Clock

Mold can begin on a wet material within 24 to 48 hours. That is why extraction is treated as an emergency rather than something that waits for an adjuster's visit.

A handful of states regulate what happens next, and the thresholds differ sharply. Texas requires licensed remediation once growth reaches 25 contiguous square feet. Louisiana requires a contractor licence above $7,500 of work. New York licenses four separate roles and bars the same firm from both assessing and remediating the same property. Most states require nothing at all. Your state hub says which applies where you are.

Drying is finished when a moisture meter says materials have returned to normal, not when a floor feels dry underfoot. Whoever you hire, this is the record to insist on, because it is what an insurer asks for:

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How much does water damage restoration cost?

Between $1,300 and $5,900 for a typical claim. By area it runs $3.75 to $7 per square foot for clean water dried without demolition, and $12 to $25 or more per square foot for Category 3 work where contaminated material has to be removed rather than cleaned.

How fast do I need to act?

Same day. Mold can begin on a wet material within 24 to 48 hours, and clean water degrades to a more expensive category the longer it stands. The cost curve here is driven by time, not by the size of the spill.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

Sudden and accidental damage, such as a burst supply line, is usually covered. Gradual leaks generally are not, and rising water from outside is excluded from standard policies entirely - that needs separate NFIP or private flood cover, which carries a waiting period.

How long does drying take?

Three to five days is typical for a contained clean-water loss with equipment monitored daily. It ends on a moisture reading rather than a fixed schedule, and a crew that promises a date before measuring is guessing.

Do I need a licensed mold contractor?

It depends on your state, and most states have no requirement at all. Texas, Louisiana, New York and the District of Columbia are among the few that license mold work, each with a different trigger. Check your state hub before assuming a licence exists to verify.
