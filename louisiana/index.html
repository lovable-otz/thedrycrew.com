Water Damage and Mold in Louisiana

The State Position

The Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors issues a Mold Remediation classification, and states that "a license is required when the value exceeds $7,500". The classification covers removal, cleaning, sanitizing, demolition or other treatment of mold-contaminated materials, including preventative measures.

Holders must complete 24 hours of board-approved training in mold remediation and assessment. Below the $7,500 threshold the work is unlicensed, which is worth knowing before treating a small quote as evidence of a credential.

Source: Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC), Mold Remediation classification. Checked 2026-08-14.

How to Check a Contractor Here

Ask for the licence number and check it against the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) register before work starts. In Louisiana a licence number you cannot look up on the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) register is not a licence. Ask separately who will issue clearance at the end, because in Louisiana that should not be the same firm doing the removal.

A licensed job costs more than an unlicensed one, and in Louisiana that is the point rather than the problem: the premium buys a protocol, a separate assessor and a clearance document. The drying work underneath it is still priced at national rates in Louisiana.

Drying itself is priced the same in Louisiana as anywhere else; the published national ranges are on the pricing page.

Does Louisiana license mold remediation?

Louisiana requires a Mold Remediation licence from the state contractor board once the job value exceeds $7,500.

Who sets the rules in Louisiana?

Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC), under the Mold Remediation classification. Position checked 2026-08-14. Louisiana can change its rules, so confirm current requirements with Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) before relying on this for a decision.
